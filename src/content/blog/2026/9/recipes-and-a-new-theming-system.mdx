## What's new on zephy.dev?

A new recipes section, and a rebuilt theming system underneath it.

### 🔪 Recipes

As someone that loves to cook, I wanted a place to share my growing collection of go-to recipes. These are a collection of dishes I grew up with, others I've picked up from working as a cook, and others I just enjoy making. 

These are live now in the new [recipes](/recipes) section — a running collection pulled live from a Notion database at build time, with cuisine/category/tag filtering and full-text search alongside the blog and portfolio. I went this route since I already kept track of my recipes on Notion and wanted to be able to keep that as the source of truth. 

The recipe pages themselves are fairly simple and easy to follow, but ingredient checklists are actually clickable, so you can tick things off as you prep (nothing saved anywhere — just a nice-to-have). Pictures, unit conversions and more coming soon.

### A real color system

The accent picker got a companion: a separate **surface** picker for backgrounds, with its own family of neutral tones — slate, gray, zinc, taupe, mauve, mist, olive, and more. Recipes gets a warm, editorial look of its own — a different font pairing, softer rounded corners, dashed underlines — built entirely from shared tokens rather than a one-off stylesheet, so the same system can back future sections too.
